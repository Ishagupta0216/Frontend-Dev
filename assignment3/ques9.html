Top 3 Travel Destinations

Kyoto, Japan

Historic temples, traditional tea houses, and beautiful cherry blossoms.

“A peaceful city full of timeless beauty.” — Anjali Kumari

Paris, France

The city of lights, famous for the Eiffel Tower, art museums, and charming cafés.

“A magical city that captures your heart with every street and corner.” — Gunjan Sirohi

London, England

A vibrant city blending historic landmarks like the Tower of London with modern attractions.

“Full of history, culture, and endless adventures.” — Ishika Gupta

Travel Tips

  • Pack layers for variable weather.
  • Always carry a copy of important documents.
  • Learn a few local phrases — it helps!
